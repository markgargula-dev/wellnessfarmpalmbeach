The idea

Wellness that feels less like an appointment and more like life.

Common Ground is not intended to replace clinical mental-health or addiction treatment. It is being developed as a complementary wellness and community experience where people can practice healthier ways of reconnecting with life.

Horse–human connection

Experiences centered around presence, communication, trust, emotional awareness, and the unique feedback horses provide.

Yoga & mindfulness

Movement, breath, and practices that support awareness, grounding, and being present with what is happening now.

Community & nature

Outdoor experiences, gatherings, volunteering, workshops, and simple opportunities to feel useful, connected, and part of something.

Founding vision

“Sometimes people don't need another appointment—they need a place to reconnect.”

Common Ground grew from founder Mark Gargula's own experience with recovery, rebuilding, yoga, community, and discovering the grounding power of the horse-human connection. The goal is to create the kind of place people can turn to when they need healthy connection, purpose, and a way back to themselves.
